The Studio

About the Work

Greytone Studio creates bespoke hand-drawn charcoal artwork for collectors who value craftsmanship, story, and individuality. Founded in Orange County, California, the studio draws inspiration from the intersection of luxury, movement, and the natural world.

Every piece begins with a conversation — a vision, a subject, a feeling. From there, the process is entirely by hand: charcoal applied with deliberate strokes, built up in layers of tone and texture until the subject emerges with the weight and atmosphere it deserves.

“Each mark is intentional. Each shadow tells part of the story.”

How It Works

The Process

From first conversation to finished, framed artwork — every commission follows the same three-step journey.

01
Consult

Share Your Vision

We begin every commission with a conversation. Tell us about your subject, the mood you want to capture, and any reference photos or inspiration you have. We'll discuss size, composition, framing preferences, and timeline. A 50% deposit initiates the work.

02
Create

Drawn by Hand

Your artwork is created entirely by hand using premium charcoal on archival paper. The process typically takes 2–4 weeks. Progress updates are shared throughout. Before the final touches are added, we send a preview so you can provide feedback and request any adjustments.

03
Deliver

Ready to Display

Upon your approval and final payment, the artwork is professionally framed using museum-quality materials and carefully packed for shipping. Your piece arrives ready to hang — protected, signed, and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity.
