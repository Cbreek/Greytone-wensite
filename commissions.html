FAQ

Frequently Asked

Have more questions? Reach out at hello@greytonestudio.com

Fill out the contact form below or email us at hello@greytonestudio.com. Share your subject, preferred size, any reference images, and your timeline. We'll follow up within 1–2 business days to discuss your vision and provide a quote.
Typical turnaround is 3–6 weeks depending on size and complexity. Rush commissions may be accommodated — please mention your deadline in your inquiry.
We draw virtually any subject — luxury vehicles, maritime subjects (yachts, sailboats, coastal scenes), lifestyle and figure studies, still life and artifacts, portraits, pets, architecture, and more. If you can photograph it, we can draw it.
High-resolution photographs work best. For vehicles, multiple angles help. For portraits and figures, clear lighting and sharpness are most important. We'll guide you through exactly what we need.
Yes — every commission includes one complimentary revision. We share a progress preview before completing the final piece so you can provide feedback. Additional major revisions may incur a small fee.
All commissions are professionally framed and carefully packed for shipping or local pickup in Orange County, California. Shipping is available nationwide. Delivery charges vary by destination and will be confirmed at the time of commission.
Yes. Fine art giclée prints of select original works are available upon request. Prints are produced on museum-quality archival paper. Please inquire for pricing and availability.
A 50% deposit is required to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon completion before shipping. We accept major credit cards, bank transfer, Venmo, and PayPal. For large or complex commissions, a custom payment schedule can be arranged.
